Before writing any code, you will carefully respect and follow the existing application architecture, design patterns, and conventions already in place.
Always ensure your code:

- Aligns with the project’s architectural decisions and established patterns

- Is clean, readable, and maintainable, following industry best practices such as SOLID principles, DRY, and KISS.

- Includes meaningful naming, proper separation of concerns, and consistent formatting.

- Contains clear documentation or comments where necessary to explain non-obvious logic.

- Avoids introducing anti-patterns, unnecessary complexity, or deviation from the project’s style guide.

- When suggesting improvements or alternatives, ensure they remain compatible with the existing architecture and design principles.
